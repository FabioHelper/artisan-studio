# SPEC-T-005: Food vocabulary and nutrient mapping v0

Task: T-005 · Goal(s): G-2, G-3 · Status tracked in `control/plan.json` (not here)

## Problem

Grams from the camera pipeline mean nothing until they become kcal and macros, and the volume
estimate needs a density to become grams. Nutrient values must be citable table rows and
commercially usable; typed-in or remembered numbers would silently become the accuracy ceiling.

## Outcome

`nutrientsFor({label, grams, state, method, oil_g})` and `massFromVolume(label, ml)` answer for ~70
common Brazilian plate foods, each number traceable to a bundled TACO or FNDDS row.

## Scope

- In: vocabulary v0, bundled source extracts with licenses, lookup module, validator, `truthNutrients` adapter.
- Out (explicitly): wiring the adapter into the eval CLI, zero-shot prompts, the gallery, calibration
  factors, the missing foods below, branded or packaged foods, TBCA (its terms forbid commercial use).

## Design

- Sources (`nutrition/sources.json`): TACO 4th ed. (Brazil; reproduction allowed with citation) and USDA
  FNDDS 2019-2020 (CC0). Only the rows used are vendored in `nutrition/data/`; each extract carries its
  origin, hashes and license. fdc.nal.usda.gov and nepa.unicamp.br are unreachable from the sandbox, so both
  came from public mirrors (URLs in the extracts). The TACO JSON agrees with an independent Excel-derived
  copy on all 597 foods.
- Classes (`nutrition/vocab.json`, schema macrofy.vocab/1, generated by `nutrition/tools/build_vocab.mjs`
  from `nutrition/tools/class_spec.mjs`, which holds no numbers): id, pt, en, facets `state`, `method`,
  `default_oil_g_per_100g`, nutrients per 100 g as eaten, `source`, `density_g_per_ml`, `density_source`.
- Nutrients come from TACO where it has the prepared food, else from FNDDS. TACO `Tr` and `NA` read as 0.
- Density = FNDDS "1 cup" portion grams / 236.6 mL, with food code, portion id and `match`
  (`exact` same food, `analog` closest FNDDS food; 19 of them). `kind: pieces` marks a cup of loose
  chopped pieces (meat, fish, egg, fruit, vegetables): a packing density, well below a solid piece's.
- Oil: 1 g oil = 1 g fat = 9 kcal. `oil_g` given is added; omitted, `default_oil_g_per_100g` applies.
  Only rice has a nonzero default (FNDDS made-with-oil minus no-added-fat fat, flagged assumption);
  TACO fried and sauteed rows already include their oil; beans stay 0 (no FNDDS oil variant to cite).
- Recipes: `salada-alface-tomate` and `salada-mista-crua` compute nutrients from ingredient classes;
  the proportions are assumptions. Feijoada and strogonoff are real TACO rows.
- `truthNutrients(item)` returns null for an unknown label or a `state` that contradicts the class.
- 18 foods (farofa, pão francês, cuscuz, coxinha, ...) are listed under `missing` with the reason:
  TACO has a row but FNDDS has no volumetric portion to derive a density from.

## Acceptance

A1 (`control/plan.json`): every class resolves to a nutrient entry, has all three facets and a density;
licenses of every bundled source are recorded. Check `nutrition-map-validate` runs
`nutrition/validate.mjs`: negative fixtures for each rule (facets, unresolved source, nutrients vs row,
Atwater 4/4/9 within 25%, density, recipes, oil basis, sources, commercial use), lookup arithmetic, then the real files.

## Risks and open questions

- Owner: are `pieces` densities acceptable for solid items, or should mass come from another route?
- Default oil for beans and vegetables is 0 until a cited Brazilian value exists; weighed `oil_g` overrides it.
- 19 densities are analogs; replace them with exact rows as sources appear.
